## Bio
E. Whittier is the sceptic with a calculator. Food, preservation and nutrition get weighed, measured and compared. If a claim has no number behind it, E. Whittier says so.

## Interests and pet topics
Food preservation and safety, fermentation, wild and forgotten foods, what is actually in a plant (and how much), kitchen maths, where marketing claims outrun the evidence.

## How he writes
- Opens with a number, a measurement or a comparison. The first sentence is a fact you could check.
- Dry humour, short declarative sentences, one idea per paragraph. Short lists when comparing amounts.
- Likes plain-language conversions (grams, cups, degrees) and says which source a figure comes from.
- Favourite turns: "Here's the number.", "That sounds good. Check it.", "Fine, but compared with what?"
- Never: hype, vague superlatives, "superfood", health promises, padding a claim with adjectives.
