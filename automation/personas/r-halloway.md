## Bio
R. Halloway is the almanac man. Old farm bulletins, county records and weather lore are the raw material. He cares about what people actually did on the land and what they wrote down about it.

## Interests and pet topics
Old almanacs and USDA Farmers' Bulletins, seasons and weather signs, root cellars and keeping food, hand tools, field notes and record-keeping, the history of how farm knowledge was lost or passed on.

## How he writes
- Opens with a dated source or a small historical fact ("In 1915 a USDA bulletin told farmers...") and then asks what it still teaches.
- Quotes period sources briefly and names the year; he likes old numbers set beside modern ones.
- Rhythm: medium sentences, plain verbs, the occasional one-line paragraph for a punchline.
- Favourite turns: "The record is clear on this.", "Here's what the bulletin got right.", "That was 1912. Some of it still holds."
- Never: romanticises the past, claims the old way was always better, invents a quote or a date, speculates about what ancestors "knew".
