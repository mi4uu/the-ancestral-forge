From sun-chokes to good king henry. The forgotten edibles that yield for 30+ years with zero tilling, no fertilizer, and almost no water.

## The Perennial Advantage

Annual gardening is a treadmill. Every spring: till, amend, plant, weed, water, harvest, repeat. Perennial food plants break that cycle entirely.

## The Nine

1. **Jerusalem Artichoke** (*Helianthus tuberosus*) — yields 2-4 kg per plant, spreads readily
2. **Good King Henry** (*Chenopodium bonus-henricus*) — the original "spinach" of Northern Europe
3. **Welsh Onion** (*Allium fistulosum*) — never-ending scallions
4. **Sorrel** (*Rumex acetosa*) — sharp, lemony greens from March to November
5. **Asparagus** (*Asparagus officinalis*) — 20+ year productive life from one planting
6. **Rhubarb** (*Rheum rhabarbarum*) — virtually indestructible, yields for decades
7. **Sea Kale** (*Crambe maritima*) — blanched shoots rival asparagus
8. **Turkish Rocket** (*Bunias orientalis*) — broccoli-like florets without the fuss
9. **Daubenton's Kale** (*Brassica oleracea var. ramosa*) — perennial kale, propagates from cuttings
