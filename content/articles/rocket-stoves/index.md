Cooking a Sunday roast on three pages of newspaper — the thermodynamics our grandfathers knew without ever writing them down.

## The Efficiency Problem

A modern gas stove is roughly 40% efficient. An open fire is 8-12%. A rocket stove? 60-70%. The difference is geometry.

## The Rocket Principle

The J-tube design forces complete combustion by:
1. Drawing air through a narrow feed tube
2. Igniting gases in an insulated combustion chamber
3. Directing all heat upward through a narrow channel

Three sheets of newspaper can boil a liter of water. A handful of twigs cooks a full meal.

## The Haybox

Once food reaches temperature, wrap the pot in insulation (historically: hay). Physics does the rest. A stew brought to boil and placed in a haybox will cook for 8 hours with zero additional fuel.
