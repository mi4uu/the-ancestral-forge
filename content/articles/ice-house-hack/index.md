Pre-electric Vermonters kept butter solid through August. The pit-and-thatch design is dead simple, and works anywhere it freezes.

## The Forgotten Cold Chain

Before refrigeration, every farm in the northern states had an ice house. Not a luxury — a necessity. They cut ice from ponds in January and it lasted through September.

## The Design

The basic ice house is:
- An 8-foot deep pit
- Lined with straw (12 inches minimum)
- Roofed with thatch at 18° pitch
- Oriented north (in the northern hemisphere)
- Small door, always shaded

## The Math

A well-built ice house loses roughly 1% of its mass per day in summer. Start with 5 tons of ice in January, you still have 2+ tons in August.
