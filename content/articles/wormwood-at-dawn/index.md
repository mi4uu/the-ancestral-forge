Every European peasant pharmacy carried Artemisia. We threw it out for ibuprofen. The receipts are now coming in.

## The Bitter Principle

Before aspirin, before ibuprofen, before any synthetic anti-inflammatory — there was wormwood. *Artemisia absinthium*. The bitter herb that every grandmother in Europe kept dried in a cloth bag above the hearth.

## Why Bitter Matters

The bitter taste receptors on your tongue trigger a cascade: bile production increases, digestive enzymes activate, gastric motility improves. The body interprets bitterness as medicine — because for 200,000 years, it was.
