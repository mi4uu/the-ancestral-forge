A 200-year-old Bavarian recipe yields more bioavailable B12, K2, and probiotics than any pharmacy capsule. Built in 30 minutes from scraps.

## The Living Pharmacy

Before the supplement industry existed, every household in central Europe maintained what they called a *Lebensglas* — a living glass. A single fermentation vessel that, when properly maintained, produced a continuous supply of B-vitamins, vitamin K2, and beneficial bacteria.

## The Recipe

You need:
- One large glass jar (2+ liters)
- Vegetable scraps (cabbage outer leaves work best)
- Non-iodized salt (2% by weight of vegetables)
- Clean water
- Time

The process takes 30 minutes of active work, then nature handles the rest.
